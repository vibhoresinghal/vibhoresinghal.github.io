Vibhore Singhal

Designer and filmmaker.

Hi, I’m Vibhore. I design products and make films. I care about the early, messy part of a thing—when the story isn’t clear yet, and the job is to make it feel obvious.

I currently design at Neo, and I’m a Senior UX Designer at Zeta. Most days I’m close to the work: flows, systems, and the details people feel before they can name them.

Outside work I shoot and cut short films. ReelsFolio is a project I built for that. I also make small tools like HueRex.
