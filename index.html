About

I’m a designer from Delhi, now living in Bangalore. I care about the details people feel before they know how to describe them—the pace of an interaction, a useful default, or a story told in the right order.

I like making things across mediums. Some become financial products used at scale, some become Figma plugins, and some become quiet films from a weekend away.
